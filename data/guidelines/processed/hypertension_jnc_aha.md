# JNC 8 and ACC/AHA Hypertension Guidelines (Key Recommendations)

Source: Eighth Joint National Committee (JNC 8) and ACC/AHA 2024 Clinical Practice Guidelines.
URL: https://www.acc.org/guidelines
License: Public clinical guideline summary.

## Section 1: Blood Pressure Targets

- For the general population aged ≥60 years: initiate treatment at systolic BP ≥150 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥90 mmHg. Target: <150/90 mmHg.
- For the general population aged <60 years: initiate treatment at systolic BP ≥140 mmHg or diastolic BP ≥90 mmHg. Target: <140/90 mmHg.
- For patients with diabetes: blood pressure target is <130/80 mmHg per ACC/AHA 2024 update.
- For patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD): blood pressure target is <130/80 mmHg regardless of age or diabetes status.
- For patients with known ASCVD or 10-year ASCVD risk ≥10%: target is <130/80 mmHg.

## Section 2: Initial Drug Therapy

- In the general non-Black population (including those with diabetes): initial therapy should include a thiazide-type diuretic, calcium channel blocker (CCB), ACE inhibitor, or ARB.
- In the general Black population (including those with diabetes): initial therapy should include a thiazide-type diuretic or CCB.
- In all patients with CKD: initial or add-on therapy should include an ACE inhibitor or ARB to improve kidney outcomes, regardless of race or diabetes status.
- Do not use an ACE inhibitor and an ARB together in the same patient due to increased risk of hyperkalemia and renal impairment.
- Beta-blockers are not recommended as first-line therapy for hypertension unless there is a specific indication (e.g., heart failure, post-MI, rate control for AF).

## Section 3: Combination Therapy

- If target BP is not achieved within 1 month of initial therapy, increase the dose or add a second agent from a different class.
- Preferred two-drug combinations: ACE inhibitor or ARB + CCB, or ACE inhibitor or ARB + thiazide diuretic.
- Avoid combining ACE inhibitors with ARBs.
- If three drugs are needed, use an ACE inhibitor or ARB + CCB + thiazide diuretic.
- Consider referral to a hypertension specialist if target BP is not achieved with three drugs including a diuretic (resistant hypertension).

## Section 4: Special Populations

- Patients with diabetes and hypertension: ACE inhibitors or ARBs are preferred, especially in the presence of albuminuria.
- Patients with CKD: ACE inhibitors or ARBs are recommended. Monitor serum potassium and creatinine within 2-4 weeks of initiation or dose change.
- Elderly patients (≥65 years): start at lower doses and titrate carefully. Monitor for orthostatic hypotension.
- Patients with heart failure: use guideline-directed medical therapy including ACE inhibitor/ARB/ARNI, beta-blocker, mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist, and diuretics as needed.
