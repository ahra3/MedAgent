# ACC/AHA Heart Failure Guidelines (Key Recommendations)

Source: ACC/AHA 2024 Guidelines for the Management of Heart Failure.
URL: https://www.acc.org/guidelines
License: Public clinical guideline summary.

## Section 1: Classification and Diagnosis

- Heart failure is classified by left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF): HFrEF (LVEF ≤40%), HFmrEF (LVEF 41-49%), HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%).
- Key symptoms: dyspnea, fatigue, exercise intolerance, fluid retention (edema, pulmonary congestion).
- Diagnosis requires clinical symptoms and signs, plus objective evidence of structural or functional cardiac abnormality.
- BNP or NT-proBNP measurement is recommended to support diagnosis and assess severity.
- Echocardiography is recommended for initial evaluation of all patients with suspected heart failure.

## Section 2: Pharmacologic Treatment of HFrEF

- Guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) for HFrEF consists of four pillars, initiated as early as possible:
  1. ACE inhibitor (or ARB, or ARNI sacubitril/valsartan) — reduces mortality and hospitalization.
  2. Beta-blocker (carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, or bisoprolol) — reduces mortality.
  3. Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA: spironolactone or eplerenone) — reduces mortality.
  4. SGLT2 inhibitor (dapagliflozin or empagliflozin) — reduces hospitalization and cardiovascular mortality.
- Diuretics (typically loop diuretics such as furosemide) are used for symptom relief in patients with fluid overload but do not improve mortality.
- Hydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate is recommended for Black patients with NYHA Class III-IV HFrEF already receiving optimal GDMT, or for patients intolerant of ACE inhibitors/ARBs.
- Titrate GDMT to target doses or maximally tolerated doses.
- Monitor renal function and electrolytes regularly, especially potassium, when using ACE inhibitors/ARBs, MRAs, and diuretics.

## Section 3: Device and Surgical Therapy

- ICD (implantable cardioverter-defibrillator) is recommended for primary prevention of sudden cardiac death in patients with LVEF ≤35% despite ≥3 months of optimal medical therapy.
- CRT (cardiac resynchronization therapy) is recommended for patients with LVEF ≤35%, NYHA Class II-IV symptoms, and QRS ≥150ms with LBBB morphology.

## Section 4: Managing Comorbidities

- Diabetes and heart failure: SGLT2 inhibitors are strongly recommended for patients with diabetes and heart failure regardless of LVEF.
- Hypertension: target BP <130/80 mmHg. Avoid non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (verapamil, diltiazem) in HFrEF.
- CKD: adjust medication doses based on eGFR. SGLT2 inhibitors provide cardiorenal benefit.
- Atrial fibrillation: rate control is preferred over rhythm control in most HF patients. Anticoagulation is recommended.
- Iron deficiency: IV iron replacement is recommended for symptomatic HFrEF patients with iron deficiency (ferritin <100 or ferritin 100-299 with TSAT <20%).
